Demo Store Website

An online store demo for a small shop that sells products online.

This demo shows a complete small online store. A visitor can browse products, open a product page, add items to a cart and complete a simple checkout form. It is the kind of website we build for shops that want to take orders online without a big, complicated system.

A real client store of this type would carry the shop's own products, prices and photos, and orders would arrive at the shop's own email or order system. Everything on this page is demo content that shows the layout and the flow.

What this demo includes

  • Product grid with photos, names and prices
  • Product page with options such as size or colour
  • Cart that updates as the visitor shops
  • Checkout form for name, address and delivery details
  • Order confirmation message after checkout
  • Contact page for questions about an order
